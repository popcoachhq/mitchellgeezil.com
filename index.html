What I built: Popcoach

Popcoach is a marketplace for fitness coaches and the people who train with them. Live on the web and the App Store, with Android on the way.

The problem. Coaches run their business across Instagram DMs, Venmo, a scheduling link and a spreadsheet. Clients have no single place to find a vetted coach, book and pay safely.

For clients. Find verified coaches nearby, book one-on-one sessions, buy tickets to pop-up events and studio classes, take courses, message coaches, and keep every booking in one place with QR check-in and Apple Wallet passes.

For coaches. A public profile, scheduling and bookings, ticketed events, class schedules, courses built from their own documents, client records, messaging, and payments with refunds and payouts handled.

Under the hood. Card and Google Pay payments, full refunds, payouts tracked to the cent, access rules on every table of customer data, and automated tests on every change before it ships.
