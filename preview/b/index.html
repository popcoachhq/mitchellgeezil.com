About me

I work with small businesses, entrepreneurs and founders to create, execute and deliver properly scoped technical solutions. In plain English.

I spent years shipping software with engineering teams at health tech companies. Software that impacted millions of patients and providers. If implemented poorly, the consequences were a matter of life or death. The margin for error was little to none. I moved from healthcare to coaching group fitness in the health and wellness industry.

Then I built my own product, Popcoach, from nothing to a full-blown iOS fitness app marketplace. I've sat on both sides of the table. I understand the importance of timelines and communication, but also the attention to detail required to ship quality products.

I'm a project manager. As technical as they come. And a total freaking nerd.

I also know slop when I see it. With today's tech, anyone can generate an app in an afternoon. Most of them should never be handed to a paying customer.

But that does not mean the available tools are bad. I use the same AI tools. But AI tools do not intrinsically build with an architectural lens. They spin up pretty front-end designs, but the user typically lacks the discipline needed to create proper backend database design, security infrastructure, monitoring and testing tools, and so much more. I can spot a vibe-coded site from a mile away.
