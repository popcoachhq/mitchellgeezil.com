iBS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The short version

iBS does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network connection.

What the app uses

  • The hinge. On an iPhone Duo the app reads the hinge angle, provided by iOS, to decide when and how to play a sound. This reading never leaves your phone.
  • Audio and haptics. The app plays sounds and vibrations. It does not use the microphone.
  • A counter. The app keeps a running count of how many times it has played, stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No data is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No third-party software development kits, trackers or advertising networks are included.
  • No account or sign-in exists.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the support link on the App Store page.
