What the numbers said

I gave Claude 90 days of my Instagram analytics and told it not to suggest anything until it had told me what was actually broken. Here's what came back.

  1. Reach was never the problem. 1,969,197 views in 90 days from about 797,000 different people, 78% of them people who don't follow me. 1,489,678 of those views were reels.
  2. 1 in 1,000. Net new followers in those 90 days: +1,792, from almost 2 million views. My three biggest reels ever (286K, 261K and 212K views) got 288, 391 and 211 follows: 1 to 1.5 per 1,000 views.
  3. The reels where I'm actually me convert 5x better. The viral sketches get shared 6,000 times and convert at 1 per 1,000. The reels where I'm a specific person convert far better on a fraction of the views: "douse yourself with chemicals before leaving the house" got 275 follows from 58,840 views (4.7 per 1,000), "home belongs to bunny" 59 from 26,252 (2.2 per 1,000). The jokes spread. The person converts.
  4. Every carousel I posted was a photo dump. Nine carousels in 90 days, seven of them with zero or one new follower. None of them gave anyone a reason to save it, send it, or follow. The one that worked was a Thanksgiving pilates flyer: 18,359 views, 4,045 shares, 1,825 profile visits, 38 follows. I've never once posted the kind of carousel that actually converts.
  5. The profile leaks. 48,194 people visited my profile in 90 days. If 1 in 8 had followed, I'd be past 10K already. They arrive from a joke and land on a bio written for fitness coaches.

Be honest with me even if the news is bad.

Then tell me exactly what to post next and why.

The two lines that matter are "do not suggest content yet" and "be honest with me even if the news is bad." Then keep using the same chat. Mine knows my numbers now.

The plan

38 days, about 156 new followers a day, no ads, no team.

  • 3 carousels a week, every one built to be saved or sent. Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays. Four lanes: the gym, building an app with AI and no developers, hot takes and lists, and my bunny. Each one is free and gated behind a keyword so I can send it to you in your DMs. Comment WINTER ARC on anything and you get this page.
  • 15 trial reels a week. From footage I already have, with a text hook. Trial reels only go to people who don't follow me, so they're the testing ground. The best one each week goes to the feed.
  • More me, less bit. The numbers say the reels where I'm a specific person are the ones people follow. So the carousel photos and the reels show the actual guy.
  • Fix the door. New bio, new pinned posts, highlights that match what brings people in, a pinned comment on every post.
  • Collab tag everyone. @popcoachhq on everything, and the people in the post. One collab with Jessi.
  • Share every number. Right here, every morning.

Why?

Because I believe in the company I'm building. I believe there's a real need for a fitness and wellness marketplace. I believe there's no better platform for distribution than Instagram. And most importantly, I believe in myself.

The longer version: I quit corporate America because I couldn't do it anymore. Not burnout exactly. I just knew I was never going to be happy building someone else's thing.

Around the same time I fell hard for group fitness. The classes, the coaches, the community. I'd never been that happy in a room full of people. And I kept noticing the same thing: the coaches running those rooms were doing it with five apps, a Venmo and a spreadsheet.

I have an IT background. I love this industry. I kept looking for the thing that sat in the middle of both, and there wasn't one. So I'm building it.

Popcoach is a business in your pocket for fitness entrepreneurs. Bookings, payments, clients, events. The pop-ups you've seen on my page all run through it.

I'm building it with no team, no budget, and no developers. Just me, way too much coffee, and AI doing the parts I can't.

So this is me proving I can build something on my own. 10,000 followers by December 31 is the first number I'm putting in public, because every coach and every client finds this through my account.

If you're a coach or you run classes, popcoach.io is where it lives. And if you're here for the growth stuff, follow along at @mitchyinmotion to see if I make it.
